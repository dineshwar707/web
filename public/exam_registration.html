📋 Examination Regulations & Instructions

✅ Permitted Items & Rules

  • Original Printed Admit Card signed by Candidate.
  • Official SRTD University Student ID Card or Govt Photo ID.
  • Transparent water bottle and transparent stationery pouch.
  • Non-programmable scientific calculator (for engineering mathematics and physics exams only).
  • Candidates must be seated in their designated hall at least 20 minutes prior to paper distribution.

🚫 Prohibited Items & Disciplinary Action

  • Smartphones, smartwatches, Bluetooth earbuds, or storage devices.
  • Written notes, chits, textbooks, or unauthorized reference materials.
  • Interchanging answer booklets or communicating with other examinees.
  • Any candidate caught indulging in unfair means (UFM) will face immediate debarment for up to 3 academic years.
